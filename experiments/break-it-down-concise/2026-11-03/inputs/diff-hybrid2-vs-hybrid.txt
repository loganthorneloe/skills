> 1. Distill. Once the full draft is written and checked, produce the delivered version at about half its reading length. Rank paragraphs by how much the reader's understanding of the answer would suffer without them, and delete or shrink the least necessary first. Merge passages that make the same point, cut background this reader already has, and shorten exhibit discussion to what to notice and its limit. Cut words, not visuals: keep every source figure, table, diagram, and interactive the draft used, and remove one only when another visual in the report shows the same data, never to save words. The cut applies to the report, not the research. Keep every claim the direct answer depends on, its mechanism, the evidence and caveats that bound it, and the field's standard name for each key concept the reader will meet elsewhere. Apply the remaining checks to the distilled version.
