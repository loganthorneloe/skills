# Source audit records

Claims
- C1: An mRNA vaccine delivers lab-made mRNA encoding one viral protein (SARS-CoV-2 spike) inside lipid nanoparticles that carry it into cells.
- C2: Inside the cell, the mRNA is read by ribosomes in the cytoplasm to make spike protein, which is shown on the cell surface.
- C3: The immune system responds to the spike: B cells make antibodies; CD4+ helper and CD8+ killer T cells respond; memory B and T cells form, giving later protection.
- C4: The vaccine mRNA does not enter the nucleus or integrate into DNA; it is broken down by normal cell processes.
- C5: Unmodified lab-made mRNA triggers innate immune alarms (TLRs) and is poorly translated; replacing uridine with modified bases (pseudouridine, N1-methylpseudouridine) reduces this and raises protein output. Licensed COVID-19 mRNA vaccines use m1Ψ.
- C6: The approach worked in people: in the Pfizer-BioNTech phase 3 trial, 8 vs 162 cases (≥7 days after dose 2), 95% efficacy.
- C7: Vaccine mRNA and spike do not vanish instantly: they were detected in lymph-node germinal centers up to ~60 days after dose 2 in some people.
- C8: One cell-culture study reported reverse transcription of vaccine mRNA into DNA in a liver cancer cell line; it did not show integration, and its relevance to people is disputed.

### Scientific background: Discoveries concerning nucleoside base modifications that enabled the development of effective mRNA vaccines against COVID-19

- byline: Gunilla Karlsson Hedestam and Rickard Sandberg (Karolinska Institutet, members of the Nobel Committee) · scientific background report · Nobel Committee for Physiology or Medicine, nobelprize.org · October 2023
- verdicts: C1 pass; C2 pass; C3 pass; C4 pass; C5 pass; C6 pass (secondary, corroborated by Polack); C7 fail (not covered); C8 fail (not covered)
- use: sections "Early work on nucleic acid- and viral vector-based vaccines" (mRNA only needs cytoplasm; "cannot integrate into the host genome"), "Delivering in vitro transcribed mRNA" (LNPs), "mRNA delivery to dendritic cells and the role of innate sensing", "The Kariko, Weissman breakthrough", Figures 2–4, "The moment was ripe" (m1Ψ, 95%/94%, memory B and T cells).
- credibility: Written by two Karolinska professors on the committee that evaluated the work, as the formal scientific justification for the prize; references primary papers throughout (Karikó 2005 Immunity, Polack 2020, Baden 2021).
- contribution: A referenced history and mechanism account: why mRNA needs only cytoplasmic access, how LNPs deliver it, how base modifications evade TLR3/7/8 and PKR and increase translation, and that licensed vaccines fully substitute uridine with m1Ψ.
- limits: Celebratory document; does not discuss persistence or dissenting lab findings. Integration statement is asserted, not demonstrated within the document. Spot-checked 95% against Polack: matches.

### Safety and Efficacy of the BNT162b2 mRNA Covid-19 Vaccine
- url: https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2034577; text read from: PubMed Central PMC7745181
- byline: Fernando P. Polack, Stephen J. Thomas, and colleagues (C4591001 Clinical Trial Group) · randomized placebo-controlled trial · New England Journal of Medicine · December 2020
- verdicts: C6 pass; C1 pass (vaccine described as "lipid nanoparticle–formulated, nucleoside-modified RNA vaccine that encodes a prefusion stabilized, membrane-anchored ... spike")
- use: Abstract results; Figure 3 (cumulative incidence after dose 1, with embedded efficacy table).
- credibility: Primary data from a 43,548-person randomized, observer-blinded trial; the regulatory pivotal trial.
- contribution: 8 cases (vaccine) vs 162 (placebo) with onset ≥7 days after dose 2 → 95% efficacy (95% credible interval 90.3–97.6); 10 severe cases after dose 1, 9 placebo vs 1 vaccine; reactogenicity mostly short-term mild-to-moderate injection-site pain, fatigue, headache.
- limits: Funded by BioNTech and Pfizer; median 2 months of safety follow-up; measured symptomatic COVID-19 against the original strain in 2020; protection waned later and against variants (Nobel background notes boosters needed). Figure 3's table uses the modified intention-to-treat population (9 vs 172, 94.8%), not the primary evaluable population (8 vs 162, 95.0%).
- read: partial (abstract, results, Figure 3, discussion skimmed) · via PMC

### mRNA vaccines — a new era in vaccinology
- url: https://doi.org/10.1038/nrd.2017.243; text read from: PubMed Central author manuscript PMC5906799
- byline: Norbert Pardi, Michael J. Hogan, Frederick W. Porter, and Drew Weissman · review article · Nature Reviews Drug Discovery · January 2018
- verdicts: C1 pass; C2 pass; C3 conditional (germinal-center mechanism described from animal studies before COVID-19 vaccines); C4 pass; C5 pass
- use: "Basic mRNA vaccine pharmacology", "Modulation of immunogenicity", "Progress in mRNA vaccine delivery" (LNP composition, endosomal release), Box 3 (germinal centres).
- credibility: Authors ran the Penn laboratory that developed nucleoside-modified mRNA–LNP vaccines (Weissman is a co-discoverer); widely used technical review.
- contribution: States mRNA is "a non-infectious, non-integrating platform" degraded by normal cellular processes; explains LNP components and endosomal release to cytoplasm; mRNA is engineered to resemble mature cytoplasmic mRNA.
- limits: Pre-dates COVID-19 vaccines; authors have patent interests in modified mRNA (conflict of interest disclosed in review). Figures under publisher copyright, not embedded.
- read: partial (sections listed; clinical tables skimmed) · via PMC author manuscript

### Immune imprinting, breadth of variant recognition, and germinal center response in human SARS-CoV-2 infection and vaccination
- url: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2022.01.018; text read from: PubMed Central PMC8786601
- byline: Katharina Röltgen, Sandra C. A. Nielsen, and colleagues (Stanford) · original research article · Cell · January 2022
- verdicts: C7 pass; C3 conditional (shows germinal center activity in vaccinee lymph nodes; observational, small n)
- use: Results section "Prolonged detection of vaccine mRNA in LN GCs and spike antigen..."; Figure 7; Limitations.
- credibility: Primary data: in situ hybridization with vaccine-specific probes and spike immunostaining on human axillary lymph-node biopsies, with non-vaccinee negative controls.
- contribution: Vaccine mRNA detected in germinal centers on days 7, 16, 37, lower but appreciable at day 60 after dose 2; spike antigen present as late as 60 days.
- limits: Seven vaccinees; biopsies not at prospective time points; observational. Shows persistence in lymph nodes, not in the nucleus or DNA. No correction found in one targeted search.
- read: partial (summary, relevant results section, Figure 7 legend, limitations) · via PMC

### Intracellular Reverse Transcription of Pfizer BioNTech COVID-19 mRNA Vaccine BNT162b2 In Vitro in Human Liver Cell Line
- url: https://doi.org/10.3390/cimb44030073; text read from: PubMed Central PMC8946961
- byline: Markus Aldén, Francisko Olofsson Falla, and colleagues (Lund University) · original research article · Current Issues in Molecular Biology (MDPI) · February 2022
- verdicts: C8 conditional (only as "one lab study in a cancer cell line reported DNA copies; integration not shown; not replicated"); C4 fail as counter-evidence for people (in vitro cancer cell line, cannot speak to vaccinated humans)
- use: Abstract; Methods 2.1 (0.5–2 µg/mL for 6–48 h); Discussion ("we do not know if DNA reverse transcribed from BNT162b2 is integrated").
- credibility: Primary lab data from a university group; modest journal; methods are qPCR and PCR on Huh7 cells.
- contribution: Reports vaccine sequence detectable as DNA in Huh7 cells within 6 h and LINE-1 changes.
- limits: Huh7 is a liver cancer line with abnormal gene expression; dose criticised as high; no integration tested; no in vivo data. Targeted search found no retraction and no independent replication.
- read: partial (abstract, methods, discussion, conclusions) · via PMC (CC BY 4.0)

## Gaps
- C4: no source directly sequences human genomes after vaccination; the "no integration" conclusion rests on mechanism (no nuclear entry, no integrase, rapid degradation) stated by expert sources, with the one contrary in vitro study not testing integration.
- C3: memory duration not quantified here; antibody levels wane (Nobel background), boosters used.
