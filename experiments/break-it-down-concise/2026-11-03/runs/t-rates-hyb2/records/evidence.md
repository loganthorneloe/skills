### E4: Stylized impulse responses
- claim: C5 (context)
- type: figure
- source: Transmission Lags of Monetary Policy: A Meta-Analysis — CNB WP 10/2012
- locator: Figure 1, p.4 (PDF p.8)
- asset: assets/hr-fig1-stylized-impulse.png
- inspected: opened at full size; both panels, axes and notes visible
- notice: Shows what "transmission lag" means: the month at which the price level's fall is deepest after a 1 pp hike; in the hump shape that is about 15 months; in the still-falling shape it is simply the last month shown.
- limits: Stylized examples, not actual estimates.
- rights: rights unstated (© Czech National Bank); embedded with attribution

### E6: How often prices change
- claim: C7 (support), C6 (context)
- type: metric
- source: Five Facts about Prices — author copy
- locator: p.1416
- asset: regular (non-sale) US consumer prices: median frequency of change 9-12% per month; implied duration 8-11 months; 1988-2005 CPI microdata
- inspected: not applicable
