# Evidence records

### E1: Cumulative COVID-19 cases after dose 1, vaccine vs placebo
- claim: C5 (support); C4 (context)
- type: figure
- source: FDA Briefing Document, Pfizer-BioNTech COVID-19 Vaccine — https://www.fda.gov/media/144245/download
- locator: Figure 2, p. 30
- asset: assets/fda-fig2-cumulative-incidence.png (rendered from PDF at 200 dpi, 1445×1167)
- inspected: opened full size; title, both axes, at-risk table, legend and footnote visible
- notice: the two curves overlap for about 10–12 days, then the vaccine curve goes flat while placebo keeps rising — protection appears once the immune response has had time to build, long after the mRNA itself would be gone.
- limits: all participants who got dose 1 (not the primary endpoint group); cutoff Nov 14, 2020; original virus; sponsor-generated plot reproduced by FDA.
- rights: U.S. federal government document; sponsor figure, rights unstated beyond that
