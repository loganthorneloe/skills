# Evidence records

### E1: From nanoparticle to spike on the cell surface
- claim: C1, C2, C3 (support)
- type: figure
- source: Nobel Committee scientific background 2023 — https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/medicine/2023/advanced-information/
- locator: Figure 4, "Spike production following mRNA vaccination and recognition of spike by B cells"
- asset: assets/nobel-figure4.jpg (2500x1361, original upload)
- inspected: opened at full size; all labels (Lipid nanoparticle, mRNA, Translation, Protein transport, Spike protein, BCR) legible; no crop.
- notice: The mRNA's path stops at ribosomes on the endoplasmic reticulum outside the nucleus; spike travels to the surface where B cell receptors bind it.
- limits: Schematic, not data. Shows only the B cell arm, not T cells.
- rights: "© The Nobel Committee for Physiology or Medicine. Ill. Mattias Karlén"; reuse terms not stated on page (rights unstated); embedded with attribution.

### E3: Trial cumulative incidence curve
- claim: C6 (support)
- type: figure+metric
- source: Polack et al. NEJM 2020 — PMC7745181
- locator: Figure 3; abstract results
- asset: assets/polack-figure3.jpg (751x735); metric: 8 vs 162 cases ≥7 days after dose 2 among ~21,700 per arm; VE 95.0% (95% credible interval 90.3–97.6)
- inspected: opened full size; axes, inset, legend and table visible.
- notice: Curves track together for about 12 days after dose 1, then the vaccine line flattens while placebo keeps rising, which is the immune response coming online.
- limits: Table in figure is mITT (9 vs 172, 94.8%); trial-era virus; median 2 months follow-up; sponsor-funded.
- rights: PMC Open Access Subset, unrestricted non-commercial re-use with acknowledgment; © Massachusetts Medical Society.

### E6: Persistence in lymph nodes
- claim: C7 (support), C4 (qualify)
- type: metric
- source: Röltgen et al. Cell 2022 — PMC8786601
- locator: Results, "Prolonged detection of vaccine mRNA..."; Figure 7
- asset: vaccine mRNA detected in lymph-node germinal centers on days 7, 16, 37, "lower but still appreciable" at day 60 after dose 2; spike antigen present as late as 60 days; n = 7 vaccinees.
- notice: Breakdown is not instant; the material that lingers is in the immune organs where antibodies are being refined.
- limits: Small, observational, not prospective time points. Figure 7 is microscopy that needs expert reading; not embedded.
- rights: Elsevier COVID-19 resource permission; not embedded.

### E7: Cell-line reverse transcription claim and its limits
- claim: C8 (qualify)
- type: quote+metric
- source: Aldén et al. 2022 — PMC8946961; Merchant 2022 — PMC9164063
- asset: Aldén: Huh7 liver cancer cells exposed to 0.5–2 µg/mL vaccine for 6–48 h; quote: "At this stage, we do not know if DNA reverse transcribed from BNT162b2 is integrated into the cell genome."
- notice: The study that is most often cited as "the vaccine changes DNA" did not test whether DNA changed.
- limits: In vitro, cancer cells, no replication found.
- rights: CC BY 4.0.
