# Evidence records

### E1: Base-modified vs unmodified mRNA (Nobel Figure 2)
- claim: C3 (support), C1 (context)
- type: figure
- source: Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine 2023 press release — https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/medicine/2023/press-release/
- locator: Figure 2
- asset: assets/nobel-fig2.jpg (2500×2210 original), assets/nobel-fig2-1200.jpg (display copy)
- inspected: opened at full size; both panels, labels, and structures legible
- notice: Same cell, two inputs. Unmodified uridine mRNA makes little protein and releases inflammatory signals; pseudouridine mRNA makes much more protein and the inflammatory signal is crossed out.
- limits: Schematic illustration, not data; the approved vaccines use 1-methylpseudouridine (Pardi Fig 1 abbreviation m1Ψ), a close relative of the pseudouridine shown.
- rights: "© The Nobel Committee for Physiology or Medicine. Ill. Mattias Karlén"; reuse terms not stated on page (rights unstated); embedded with attribution.

### E2: What makes an injected mRNA vaccine work (Pardi Figure 3)
- claim: C4 (support), C3 (context)
- type: figure
- source: Pardi et al. 2018, Nat Rev Drug Discov — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5906799/
- locator: Figure 3
- asset: assets/pardi-fig3.jpg (638×623, PMC largest)
- inspected: opened; all three numbered boxes and labels legible
- notice: Three steps in order: (1) cells at the injection site, including antigen-presenting cells, take up particle-wrapped mRNA and make antigen; (2) dendritic cells mature and travel to the lymph node; (3) there, T follicular helper cells and germinal-centre B cells are activated.
- limits: Drawn for intradermal injection (COVID vaccines are intramuscular); 2018 review calls step 3 poorly understood at the time.
- rights: Author manuscript in PMC; Nature copyright; rights unstated for reuse; embedded with attribution.

### E3: Vaccine mRNA and spike in lymph node germinal centres over time (Röltgen Figure 7)
- claim: C5 (support), C4 (support)
- type: figure+metric
- source: Röltgen et al. 2022, Cell — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8786601/
- locator: Figure 7, panels A–E; Results "Localization of SARS-CoV-2 proteins and vaccine mRNA in LNs"
- asset: assets/roltgen-fig7.jpg (766×967)
- inspected: opened; panels A–L visible, small text readable at full size
- notice: Panel B: red dots (vaccine-mRNA probe) and brown granules (spike) in germinal centres at days 7–60 after dose 2; panel D: probe spots per germinal centre peak around day 16–37 and drop sharply by day 42 but are not zero.
- limits: Few people, biopsies at irregular times; lymph nodes on the injected side only; presence of mRNA says nothing about DNA.
- rights: Elsevier COVID-19 resource centre permission: unrestricted research re-use with acknowledgement while the centre remains active.

### E4: Cumulative COVID-19 cases, vaccine vs placebo (Polack Figure 3)
- claim: C6 (support)
- type: figure+metric
- source: Polack et al. 2020, NEJM — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7745181/
- locator: Figure 3 and its embedded table; Efficacy section
- asset: assets/polack-fig3.jpg (751×735); metric: 8 vs 162 cases ≥7 days after dose 2, participants without prior infection (n=36,523), VE 95.0% (95% credible interval 90.3–97.6)
- inspected: opened; both curves, inset, axis labels, and table rows visible
- notice: The lines track together for about 12 days after dose 1, then the vaccine line flattens while placebo keeps climbing — the delay is the time the immune system needs to build its response.
- limits: Symptomatic, PCR-confirmed disease only; ~2 months median follow-up; ancestral strain; Pfizer-sponsored.
- rights: PMC Open Access Subset, "unrestricted re-use, except commercial resale ... with acknowledgment of the original source."

### E5: Waning effectiveness, 1–7 months after final dose (Feikin Figure 2)
- claim: C7 (support)
- type: figure+metric
- source: Feikin et al. 2022, Lancet — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8863502/
- locator: Figure 2; Summary Findings
- asset: assets/feikin-fig2.jpg (779×845, largest PMC version); metric: average fall 1→6 months of 21.0 points (infection), 24.9 (symptomatic), 10.0 (severe), all ages
- inspected: opened; panels and legends legible, small; link to source for full view
- notice: Left columns (severe disease) stay high near 80–100%; right columns (any infection) slope downward with time.
- limits: Pre-Omicron; mixes four vaccines (Pfizer and Moderna lines are the mRNA ones); several studies at serious risk of bias.
- rights: CC BY 4.0.
