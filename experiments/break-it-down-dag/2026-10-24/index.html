Graph audit

All four DAGs are structurally valid; their dependency claims need repair. Edge quality scored 2–3/5, and evidence alignment scored 3/5 throughout. The reports often supplied concepts or sensible ordering missing from their own graphs.

Concrete failures include levels → serial reversing a needed definition; bits → mod making message representation a prerequisite for remainders; partial citations marked fully supported; and duplicate or indistinguishable revision states. All rendered graphs match their JSON, but their mobile labels are too small.

Recommendation: keep this as an optional experiment. Tighten edge necessity, separate optional branches, leave partial evidence visible, and preserve actual revision changes before another comparison. The evaluated skill and reports remain frozen.
